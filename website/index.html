Model card

What the model assumes—and how it can fail

External likelihood

SilverAvgₜ* ~ Student-t(θₜ*, σaggregate)
t* = latest published date only

Silver's daily history is highly autocorrelated. The latest-only invariant prevents pseudo-replication, while a robust likelihood limits conflict with fundamentals.

Race posterior

marginᵣ ~ Student-t(fundamentalsᵣ, σᵣ)
SilverAvgLatestᵣ updates marginᵣ

Races without a validated Silver D-v-R average remain at their fundamentals posterior. Shared national and regional draws preserve chamber correlation; a historical common-error floor prevents false precision.

Diagnostics

Loading…

Analytic updates do not have MCMC R-hat. Historical parameter fits must meet R-hat < 1.01, bulk ESS > 400, and zero divergences.

Backtest evidence

Loading…

The rolling-origin test supports the robust update and error floor. It does not directly validate Silver's maintained averages, for which a comparable public historical race archive is unavailable.

Calibration · final 60 days

Points compare forecast probability with observed Democratic win rate. The diagonal is perfect calibration. This is supporting Senate update evidence, not a provider-specific Silver average backtest.

Data provenance

Polling likelihoods: Silver Bulletin maintained averages. Approval prior: VoteHub. Candidates: FEC. Incumbents: Clerk of the House. Snapshots retain URL, fetch time, source notice, and checksum.

Known limitations

  • Silver's weighting model is external and is not reproduced or independently audited here.
  • Ambiguous, third-party, and uncovered races stay fundamentals-only.
  • The historical promotion evidence is Senate-only and predates this external-average input.
  • No MRP, fundraising, endorsements, or subjective candidate scores.
